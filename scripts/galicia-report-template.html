How to read this

Climate cells summarize approximately 5 km source data. Model values are area-weighted within each 10 km cell before taking the ensemble median. At least 80% of the clipped cell must have valid data.

The historical climate baseline is 1971–2000, not current observations. Mid-century is 2041–2070. River-flood dates differ and appear above the map. Model spread is not a confidence interval or a probability of being safe.

Heavy rainfall does not measure flood depth. Dry spells do not measure water security. Heatwave duration is relative to each place’s own historical heat threshold.

Sources: AdapteCCa / AEMET / CSIC; WRI Aqueduct Floods v2; boundary © Eurostat GISCO, NUTS 2024. See the adjacent CSV, GeoJSON and provenance JSON for data and reproducibility.

Coastal-flood samples are stored separately and do not cover all Galicia. Fire, landslides, wind and other hazards are not assessed here. A blank or zero layer cannot establish overall low hazard.
