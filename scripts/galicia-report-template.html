How to read this

AdapteCCa climate cells summarize approximately 5 km source data. Model values are area-weighted within each 10 km cell before taking the ensemble median. At least 80% of the clipped cell must have valid data.

The AdapteCCa historical baseline is 1971–2000, not current observations. Its mid-century window is 2041–2070. Other sources use different windows, shown above the map. Model spread is not a confidence interval or a probability of being safe.

Heavy rainfall does not measure flood depth. Dry spells do not measure water security. Heatwave duration is relative to each place’s own historical heat threshold.

Summer fire weather, regional context

FIRE-RES provides an accessible mid-century Fire Weather Index projection. Its source grid is about 0.5°, roughly 40 by 55 km here. We show that grid directly: this layer cannot distinguish neighbouring 10 km places. FWI describes weather favourable to fire, not ignition probability or the area that will burn.

These files contain published ensemble means without the model members or their spread. Change is a difference of supplied means. Scenario means may use different model sets, so differences between them cannot be attributed solely to emissions. June–August does not cover Galicia's whole fire season.

Vegetation, ignition, terrain and firefighting also affect actual fires. This index alone cannot identify places with low wildfire hazard.

River flooding: compare with official maps

Aqueduct represents larger rivers and excludes flood defences. SNCZI combines individual studies with different dates and assumptions. Source resolution, mapped river networks and study coverage all affect the comparison.

Removing depths below 10 cm is a sensitivity check, not a validated correction. The remaining Aqueduct-only extent is not a measured false-positive area.
